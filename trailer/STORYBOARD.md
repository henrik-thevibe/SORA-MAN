## Revision 3 rules
- **Palette:** keep the happy hues and only darken them for contrast. Sunbursts are Sora gold (open, title, end), game blue (versus), sunset magenta (sunset) and remix purple, tinted over the neutral `rays-white` texture (`HUE` in `src/shots.js`). Painted mazes use the game's SORA maze blue with gold pellets.
- **Post pass** (`src/main.js`, helpers in `src/core.js`): bloom, a warm soft-light grade, a chromatic-aberration punch on every slam, boom and title sting (`TIMELINE.PUNCH`), vignette, grain, and the letterbox during the open.
- **Motion blur:** 5 sub-frames over a 180° shutter inside `TIMELINE.BLUR` windows (transitions, whips, slams, the title run, the chase, the stinger). Add `?noblur` to the studio URL to skip it while scrubbing.
- **Master:** `--encode` runs a two-pass `loudnorm` to −14 LUFS, −1.5 dBTP.

## Revision 4: visual language
- **Callouts** on the gameplay come from the per-frame track: a YOU arrow on Sora, a neon trail, rings and name tags on the ghost that matters (CLAUDE in the near miss, the target in each showcase), a dashed ring on the power-up, 1UP–4UP rings in versus, and a GLITCH arrow on the rising line. They are set by the `callouts` field on each segment.
- **Sound stickers:** pop-art bubbles (GULP!, WAKA WAKA!, ZAP!, BOOM!, POWER UP!, CHOMP!) on real game events, plus WAKA! at the pre-drop chomp and GULP! in the stinger.
- **Pellet-trail reveals:** Sora eats the game's own pellet chips across a line to reveal FOUR AIs. ONE MAZE., the GPU line, CAN YOU OUTRUN CLAUDE?, the REMIX feature line and PLAY FREE IN YOUR BROWSER (`TIMELINE.REVEALS`).
- **Break the frame:** Claude leans over the classic screen, Sora grips the REMIX screen, clings to the SUNSET screen and peeks at each AFTER panel, and the versus stars overlap the arena. Every screen lights the room in its footage colour.
- **Backgrounds:** a pellet grid for REMIX, a synthwave horizon for SUNSET and an arena checker for VERSUS. Sunbursts only for the character, title and end moments.
- **The game's own art** is used for every pellet in the film: chips and GPU power pellets in bursts, lanes, chomp crumbs and the painted maze.

## Revision 5: legibility pass
- **REMIX** is four hero showcases plus a two-bar montage; the grey BEFORE panel is gone. Everything after REMIX moved 6 bars earlier.
- **Classic chain** uses one energizer run (clip frames 512–778), so the badges read 200, 400, 800, 1600.
- **No collisions:** PLAY NOW! and POWERED UP! sit clear of the titles; callout lines take their own heights; the SUNSET words sit in columns either side of the cabinet; VERSUS cuts before the results screen.
- **Title-safe callouts:** the YOU tag flips below Sora, and ghost name tags flip above, whenever they would leave the frame (`fx.js`).

## Revision 5, phase 2: pacing
- The cold open is 4 bars (the first recognisable image lands at 3.75 s). Everything from the Sora reveal on moved 1 bar earlier.
- 256 SCREEN CLEAR is a pure additive white flash (2 frames held, 10-frame decay) with no sticker over it.
- Screens hand off: each showcase screen slides out as the next whips in, the REMIX intro screen leaves with its words, and giant Sora clears the frame on the chase's cut.
- Words no longer ride the whips (so the motion blur never smears them); descriptions rise into place.
- VERSUS: red borders sweep inward while THE ARENA CLOSES IN. is up.
- The stinger holds on the wink for about 0.75 s before the fade.

## Revision 5, phase 3: visual polish
- Classic: the painted maze behind the cabinet is blurred 8 px and darkened to about 40% luma, so the gameplay reads as the subject.
- FOUR AIs. ONE MAZE.: Claude leads a size up, and Grok has a cream rim light against the dark maze.
- Grok's card is violet grey (`#6a5c8c` / `#3a3052`) instead of near-black.
- Title: Sora starts his dot run on screen.
- Rapid: the checklist panel grows a line at a time from its top edge; each line's icon ticks with a small pop.
- Tribute: the dates use the supporting face at 38 px. The end-card disclaimer is 26 px.

## Revision 5, phase 4: final polish
- **Stickers are rationed** (`stickerAllowed` in `shots.js`). In each shot only the first event of each kind gets one, never within two bars of the previous sticker. Each sits at least 1.5 tiles off the action, on the side away from travel, with its tail pointing back. They pop in over 4 frames with about 12% overshoot, hold 10 frames and scale out over 6.
- **Impacts** (`TIMELINE.IMPACTS`): a 2-frame, 6 px whole-picture shake plus a chromatic punch on the 1600 eat, each showcase impact, 256, DELETED and the stinger's GULP. The 1600 eat also gets a 3-frame hit-stop.
- **Score dynamics** (`audio/score.mjs`):
  - The verses sit about 4 dB under the peaks (`DYN`).
  - A one-beat breath before SUNSET.
  - A two-beat low-pass sweep (8 kHz to 400 Hz) into DELETED, then a silent bar with only the roar and a sub drop.
  - A reverse-cymbal swell into VERSUS, whose first bar is drums and bass only.
  - The open's static is 6 dB quieter with a -6 dB high shelf.
  - Loudness range is about 6.4 LU at -14 LUFS.

## Revision 6: second review
- **VERSUS is a new capture where Sora wins.**
  - Seed 113. Sora plays with the game's own bot brain (`AstraModes.versusBot`), so the match is fair and replays frame for frame in node.
  - He eats Nova and Lyra early, and in the last bar the glitch takes the other three while he holds the sanctuary.
  - The cut goes to the game's own result screen: 1 SORA, STANDING.
  - The versus tags now carry the stars' names instead of 1UP to 4UP.
- **Game change:** VERSUS bots always have a plan and never stand still (see the README). Before, spirits stood still in 36% of samples and energised bots in 56%.
- **Showcase framing:** showcase crops may run past the maze edge (`free` in `footage.screen`), so Sora stays mid-frame even on the bottom row.
- **Label placement:** each callout tries a list of candidate spots and takes the first that is inside title-safe and clear of the labels already placed. The YOU tag sits beside Sora, where the game's own pop-ups don't reach.
- **Rapid:** each cut matches its line: a maze, the maze editor carving corridors (`capture/editor.mjs`), co-op, the wardrobe, the achievements tab and the menu.
- **Exits:** small lines leave before their headline (REMIX, VERSUS).
- **Tribute:** FOR SORA at 62 px; the dates at 32 px and 75% opacity.
- **End card:**
  - After the stinger, the four ghosts close ranks around the centre.
  - PLAY NOW! and GULP! sit inside title-safe.
  - There is no URL on the card; the link goes in the posts the trailer is shared with.
- **Score:** SUNSET sits at 0.85, so the title drop, chase and end card stay the loudest moments.
